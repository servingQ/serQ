# Collapse and recovery of vLLM's prefix cache: the study

Generated by `tools/metastability/study.py`; simulation of `examples/multi-turn/vllm.sq` as committed, not a measurement.
Mean ± 95 % CI over seeds 1–5 (Student t). 34000 blocks of 16 tokens: about 32.6 contexts of K0 + n + o = 16 700 tokens. Windows of 25 s. A *full hit* is an admission that reused at least K0 = 16000 tokens; a window is *collapsed* below 0.1 full hits, and a collapse is the first 4 collapsed windows in a row. *Recompute* is the prefill tokens an admission computes. *Goodput* counts the turns whose TTFT is at most 2 s. A row's means are over the last half of its horizon.

## E4: open conversations (`open_burst.sq`, p = 0.9, Z = 4 s; the burst 1.5 conversations/s for 60 s from 1000 s)

The arms:

- `lru`: vLLM's free queue, `evict lru;` (as committed)
- `full`: a waiting turn's prefix evicted last, `evict by (waiting);`
- `partial`: the same for 10 s after its release, `evict by (waiting * (age < 10));`
- `compute2x`: half the compute per scheduled token, `let a = 1e-5;`
- `kv1.5x`: 1.5 times the KV blocks, `--blocks 51000`
- `live30`: at most 30 conversations in, `--max_live 30`
- `live25`: at most 25 conversations in, `--max_live 25`

`collapsed` counts the seeds with a collapse anywhere in the run, `at end` those whose last 500 s are all collapsed, `growing` those whose backlog (waiting turns plus conversations waiting to enter) grew from the third quarter of the run to the fourth by more than 5 and a fifth. `door` is the conversations waiting to enter (`--max_live` only). ## The band
